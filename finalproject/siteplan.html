Silver Sage Motel

Comfort Inspired by Nature

Site Name

Silver Sage Motel

I selected this name because it connects directly with the motel's identity and location. "Silver" represents the silver-gray exterior color of the building, while "Sage" represents the natural sagebrush and western landscape commonly associated with Utah.

The name feels professional, memorable, and authentic for a motel located in Duchesne, Utah.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to create a modern and trustworthy online presence for Silver Sage Motel. The site will help travelers learn about the motel, view room options, explore amenities, understand the location, and find contact or reservation information.

The website will be designed to make visitors feel safe and confident before choosing a place to stay in Duchesne, Utah.

Scenarios

  • What room options are available at Silver Sage Motel?
  • What amenities does the motel offer for travelers?
  • How can I contact the motel or request a reservation?
  • Is the motel located near outdoor attractions or main roads in Duchesne, Utah?

Color Scheme

Silver Gray #8A8D91
Dark Charcoal #343A40
Sage Green #7C826B
Warm White #F5F5F0

Silver Gray will be used as a main brand color because it reflects the motel's exterior. Dark Charcoal will be used for headings, navigation, and footer areas. Sage Green will be used as a natural accent color for buttons, highlights, and important details. Warm White will be used for page backgrounds to create a clean, calm, and welcoming design.

Typography

Headings: Georgia, serif. This font gives the website an elegant, professional, and welcoming style.

Body Text: Arial, sans-serif. This font is easy to read and works well for paragraphs, navigation links, cards, and general website content.
