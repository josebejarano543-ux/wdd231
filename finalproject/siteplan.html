Travel Nest Motel Site Plan

Site Name

Travel Nest Motel

I chose this name because it sounds welcoming, comfortable, and trustworthy for travelers. The word "Nest" gives the idea of a safe and relaxing place to stay.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide travelers with clear and trustworthy information about a modern motel. The site will show room options, amenities, location, safety features, and contact or reservation information. The goal is to help visitors feel confident before choosing a place to stay.

Scenarios

  • What room options are available and what amenities are included?
  • How can I contact the motel or request a reservation?
  • Is the motel safe, clean, and close to important local places?

Color Scheme

Primary Color: #1E3A5F

This dark blue color will be used for the header, navigation, footer, and main headings. It gives the site a professional and trustworthy feeling.

Accent Color: #D4A017

This gold color will be used for buttons, highlights, and important call-to-action elements. It helps the website feel warm and welcoming.

Background Color: #F7F7F7

This light background color will be used for the body of the website to keep the design clean and easy to read.

Typography

Headings: Georgia, serif. This font will be used for titles and headings because it gives the website a professional and elegant style.

Body Text: Arial, sans-serif. This font will be used for paragraphs, lists, navigation, and general content because it is clean and easy to read.
